## PC Controls and Keybinds

PC players in REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE use a traditional keyboard and mouse setup that prioritizes precision and quick access to tactical options. The mouse handles your primary combat actions while the keyboard gives you instant access to movement modifiers and weapon management.

**Combat Controls**: Left-click fires your weapon, while right-click brings up your aim down sights view for improved accuracy. The R key handles reloads, and V switches between fire modes—essential for adapting between full-auto, burst, and semi-automatic fire depending on your engagement distance.

**Movement and Positioning**: Hold Shift to sprint across the map quickly, though this makes you more vulnerable. The C key toggles between standing, crouching, and going prone, letting you reduce your profile and hold angles more effectively. Q and E lean left and right respectively, allowing you to peek around corners while minimizing your exposure.

**Weapon Management**: Number keys 1 through 9 let you switch between weapons in your loadout instantly. The G key serves as your interact button for objectives and environmental actions.

## Console Controls Layout

Console players use a controller scheme that maps REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE controls to familiar shooter button layouts. The game adapts the tactical mechanics to work smoothly with a gamepad.

**Trigger and Bumper Actions**: RT (right trigger) fires your weapon, while LT (left trigger) aims down sights. RB (right bumper) cycles through fire modes, giving you quick access to change between automatic and semi-automatic fire without entering menus.

**Face Buttons**: The X button reloads your weapon, B handles your stance changes between standing, crouching, and prone, and Y switches between your primary and secondary weapons. LB (left bumper) also provides weapon switching functionality.

**Stick and D-Pad Controls**: Click L3 (left stick) to sprint. The D-Pad controls your lean—press left or right to peek around corners tactically. The game uses these inputs to bring the same tactical movement options from PC to console seamlessly.

## Mastering Tactical Movement Controls

The tactical movement system in REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE sets it apart from simpler Roblox shooters. The combination of leaning, crouching, and prone positions gives you multiple ways to approach every engagement.

**Leaning**: Using Q and E on PC or the D-Pad on console, you can peek around corners while keeping most of your body in cover. This is crucial for checking angles without fully exposing yourself to enemy fire. The lean mechanic combines with the aiming system to create realistic peek-and-shoot gameplay.

**Stance Management**: The C key on PC or B button on console cycles through standing, crouching, and prone positions. Crouching reduces your profile and improves accuracy, making it ideal for holding angles. Going prone makes you the smallest possible target and provides maximum stability, though it limits your mobility. Learning when to use each stance is key to surviving longer rounds.

**Sprint Timing**: The sprint function (Shift on PC, L3 on console) lets you cover ground quickly between cover points, but you're vulnerable while sprinting. The game rewards players who sprint strategically—moving quickly when safe, then transitioning to a combat-ready stance before engaging.

## Combat Controls and Fire Modes

REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE emphasizes realistic weapon mechanics through its combat controls. The fire mode system in particular requires tactical thinking about your ammunition and engagement range.

**Aiming Down Sights**: Right-click on PC or LT on console brings up your weapon's sights, dramatically improving accuracy at the cost of movement speed and field of view. The game rewards players who take time to aim properly rather than hip-firing indiscriminately.

**Fire Mode Switching**: The V key on PC or RB on console cycles through available fire modes for your current weapon. Most weapons offer multiple modes—full-automatic for close quarters, burst fire for medium range, and semi-automatic for long-distance precision. Managing your fire mode based on the situation conserves ammunition and improves your effectiveness.

**Reload Management**: The R key on PC or X button on console reloads your current weapon. Since the game focuses on realism, you need to find cover before reloading rather than reloading in the open. The reload mechanic encourages you to track your ammunition and plan reloads during safe moments.
