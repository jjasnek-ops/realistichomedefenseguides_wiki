### PC Controls

PC players have access to the most precise control scheme using mouse and keyboard:

- **Fire**: Left Click
- **Aim**: Right Click
- **Reload**: R
- **Fire Mode**: V
- **Sprint**: Shift
- **Crouch / Prone**: C
- **Lean**: Q (left), E (right)
- **Switch Weapon**: Number keys 1-9
- **Interact**: G

The mouse-and-keyboard setup allows for rapid weapon switching using number keys and precise aiming with the mouse. The lean mechanic using Q and E keys enables players to peek around corners while minimizing exposure, a crucial tactical advantage in firefights.

### Console Controls

Console players use controller inputs optimized for gamepad play:

- **Fire**: RT (Right Trigger)
- **Aim**: LT (Left Trigger)
- **Reload**: X
- **Fire Mode**: RB (Right Bumper)
- **Sprint**: L3 (Left Stick Click)
- **Crouch / Prone**: B
- **Lean**: D-Pad
- **Switch Weapon**: Y, LB (Left Bumper)
- **Interact**: X

Console controls follow standard shooter conventions, making the game accessible to players familiar with other console shooters. The trigger-based firing system provides good feedback, while the D-pad lean mechanic allows tactical positioning without removing your thumb from the movement stick.

### Mobile Controls

Mobile players access on-screen buttons that can be customized in the Settings menu. The mobile control scheme adapts the full feature set to touchscreen devices, ensuring mobile players aren't at a disadvantage. Players should explore the Settings menu to adjust button positions, sizes, and sensitivity to match their preferred playstyle and device screen size.

## Tactical Gameplay Tips

The fire mode control (V on PC, RB on console) allows you to switch between firing modes on weapons that support it. Different situations call for different firing modes—use automatic fire for close-quarters engagements and single-shot or burst modes for long-range precision. Master the reload timing (R on PC, X on console) to ensure you're never caught empty in a firefight.

### Movement and Positioning

The sprint, crouch, and prone mechanics provide different movement speeds and profile sizes. Sprinting gets you to cover quickly but makes you more visible and audible. Crouching reduces your profile and improves accuracy. Going prone makes you the smallest target but limits mobility. Learn when to use each stance based on the tactical situation.

The lean mechanic is particularly powerful in REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE. Using Q and E on PC or the D-Pad on console, you can peek around corners while exposing minimal body area. This allows you to gather information and engage enemies while maintaining cover advantage.

- Switching weapons (1-9 on PC, Y/LB on console) rather than reloading in emergencies
