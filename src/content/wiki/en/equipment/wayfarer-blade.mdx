## Where to find it

Speak to the keeper at Wayfarer’s Rest after the training sentinel routine. The weapon is a guaranteed tutorial reward in this imaginary example; no random drop is involved.

## Upgrade priorities

Upgrade once before entering the Old Grove. Save the remaining fragments until you know whether the move set fits your playstyle. The [beginner route](/guides/first-steps/) connects the workbench and grove campfire.

## Build synergy

The [Trailkeeper loadout](/builds/trailkeeper/) pairs this weapon with a guard skill and a short evasive step. It is designed as a readable example of equipment and skill data working together.
