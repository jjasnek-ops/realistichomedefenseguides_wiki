## How to use this ranking

A tier is a quick comparison, not a substitute for understanding a weapon. This fictional ranking assumes one player using early equipment against ordinary PvE enemies. Different assumptions can change the order.

## Compare the tradeoffs

| Weapon | Strength | Tradeoff | Suitable for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wayfarer’s Blade | Short recovery | Modest reach | Learning encounter timing |
| Oakbow | Distance control | Weak close response | Patient positioning |
| Stoneguard | Defensive flexibility | Slow pursuit | Safer first attempts |
| Traveler’s Spear | Reach | Long recovery | Familiar encounters |

## Why the blade leads

The [Wayfarer’s Blade](/equipment/wayfarer-blade/) has the fewest constraints in this example. A missed opening costs less when a weapon recovers quickly, leaving time to reposition.

## When to choose something else

Choose the bow when distance makes the encounter easier to read. Choose the shield when learning a new attack pattern is more useful than maximizing damage. The [combat guide](/guides/combat-basics/) explains the habits that matter across all four options.
