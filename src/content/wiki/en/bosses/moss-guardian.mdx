## Prepare for the encounter

Rest at the grove campfire and equip a weapon with a short recovery. The [Wayfarer’s Blade](/equipment/wayfarer-blade/) is the straightforward demonstration option. The [Trailkeeper build](/builds/trailkeeper/) adds a little defensive flexibility.

## Attack patterns

| Attack | Tell | Recommended response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Root sweep | Right arm moves behind the body | Dodge toward the sweeping arm |
| Stonefall | Both hands touch the ground | Leave the pale ring, then wait |
| Bark burst | Moss briefly brightens | Move out and let the pulse finish |

### Phase one: learn the sweep

Keep a medium distance and wait for the root sweep. After dodging through it, land two light attacks and return to neutral. There is no need to force a third hit.

### Phase two: respect the follow-up

Below half health, Stonefall gains a second pulse. The visual ring remains on the ground until the second pulse ends. Attack only after it disappears.

<Accordion title="A safe response to the second pulse">
Walk away after the first pulse instead of immediately dodging back in. Keeping your dodge available gives you another answer if the guardian turns toward you.
</Accordion>

## Recommended strategy

1. Stay near the edge of the clearing, keeping the center free for repositioning.
2. Bait the root sweep and punish with a short combo.
3. Stop attacking as soon as both hands move toward the ground.
4. In phase two, count both pulses before returning.
