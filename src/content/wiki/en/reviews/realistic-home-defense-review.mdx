### Tactical Movement Options

Movement in REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE goes beyond basic navigation. The game implements:

- **Leaning**: Players can lean left or right to peek around corners while minimizing their exposure
- **Crouching**: Reduces the player's profile and can improve stability

- **Sprinting**: For quick repositioning when needed

These movement options work together to support defensive strategies. Holding angles becomes a viable tactic when combined with leaning, while going prone can provide crucial advantages in long sightlines.
