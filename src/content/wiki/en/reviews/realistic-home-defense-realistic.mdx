## Complete Control Schemes

REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE supports multiple platforms with tailored control schemes for PC, console, and mobile players. Understanding these controls is essential for tactical gameplay and survival.

### PC Controls

PC players use keyboard and mouse combinations for precise aiming and quick actions:

- Fire weapon: Left Click
- Aim down sights: Right Click
- Reload: R key
- Toggle fire mode: V key
- Sprint: Shift key
- Crouch or go prone: C key
- Lean left or right: Q and E keys
- Switch weapons: Number keys 1-9
- Interact with objects: G key

### Console Controls

Console players using controllers have a mapped layout optimized for gamepad play:

- Fire weapon: RT (Right Trigger)
- Aim down sights: LT (Left Trigger)
- Reload: X button
- Toggle fire mode: RB (Right Bumper)
- Sprint: L3 (Left Stick click)
- Crouch or go prone: B button
- Lean: D-Pad directions
- Switch weapons: Y button and LB (Left Bumper)
- Interact with objects: X button

### Mobile Controls

Mobile players access on-screen buttons that can be customized through the Settings menu. This allows players to adjust the layout and sensitivity to their personal preferences, ensuring competitive gameplay on touch devices.

The cross-platform control support ensures that REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE remains accessible to the broad Roblox player base regardless of their preferred gaming platform.
