REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE is a tactical home-defense shooter on Roblox that emphasizes realistic weapon mechanics. Released on October 8, 2026 by IBA Community, the game features authentic aiming down sights, reloads, and fire-mode switching combined with tactical movement options.

## Weapon Controls

### PC Controls

| Action | Keybind |
|--------|---------|
| Fire | Left Click |
| Aim Down Sights | Right Click |
| Reload | R |
| Fire Mode Toggle | V |
| Switch Weapon | 1–9 |

### Console Controls

| Action | Button |
|--------|--------|
| Fire | RT |
| Aim Down Sights | LT |
| Reload | X |
| Fire Mode Toggle | RB |
| Switch Weapon | Y / LB |

### Mobile Controls

Mobile players use on-screen buttons for firing, aiming, reloading, and movement. These controls are editable in the game's Settings menu, allowing customization for different device preferences and screen sizes.

## Weapon Mechanics

REALISTIC HOME DEFENSE focuses on realistic weapon handling that requires tactical awareness and precision. The game includes dedicated mechanics for aiming down sights, manual reloading, and fire-mode selection—features that distinguish it from arcade-style shooters on the Roblox platform.

The fire-mode toggle (V on PC, RB on console) allows players to switch between firing modes during combat, adapting weapon behavior to different tactical situations and engagement distances.

## Getting Started with Weapons

When you enter a match from the official Roblox page, you'll spawn with your loadout. Take time to familiarize yourself with the weapon switching system and practice the core mechanics: aiming down sights, reloading under pressure, and toggling fire modes as situations demand.

The weapon switching system supports slots 1 through 9 on PC, giving tactical loadout flexibility. Console players use Y or LB to cycle through available weapons, while mobile players access weapons through on-screen buttons configured in Settings.
