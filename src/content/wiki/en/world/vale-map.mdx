## A route with room to explore

Follow the river instead of cutting across the ridge. In this imaginary setting, the river path connects the training camp, a few upgrade materials and the workbench. The route is described in the [first steps guide](/guides/first-steps/).

### Before the Old Grove

Check your weapon and supplies. The [guardian encounter guide](/bosses/moss-guardian/) explains the two attacks worth recognizing before you enter.
