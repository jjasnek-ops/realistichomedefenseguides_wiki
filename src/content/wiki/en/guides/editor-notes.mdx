## Public but excluded from discovery

This page demonstrates the noindex policy. It renders at a real URL with a noindex meta tag, but is excluded from category listings, recent updates, related guides, alternate-language declarations, sitemap and Pagefind.

The distinct verification phrase **quiet-orchid-reference** is used by the search exclusion check.
