## A three-part practice routine

1. Spend one attempt observing without attacking. Learn the difference between a windup and a swing.
2. Dodge three attacks in a row, returning to a neutral distance after each one.
3. Add one light attack after a successful dodge. Build the combo only when the response feels reliable.

## Read attack tells

| Tell | Response | Safe opening |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raised shoulder | Dodge across the swing | One short combo |
| Both hands planted | Step away from the ground strike | One ranged attack |
| Enemy steps backward | Wait for the follow-up | No immediate punish |

## Manage stamina

Stop attacking when the bar falls below half. A full damage rotation is rarely worth losing the ability to move. The [Trailkeeper build](/builds/trailkeeper/) demonstrates a forgiving loadout for practicing this rhythm.
