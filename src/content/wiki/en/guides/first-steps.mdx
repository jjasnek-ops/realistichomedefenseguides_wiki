## Learn the rhythm of combat

Every attack has a preparation, an active swing and a recovery. **Watch the preparation, dodge the swing, and attack during recovery.** Trying to attack through all three stages is the easiest way to run out of stamina.

### Keep one dodge in reserve

Spend no more than half your stamina on an opening combo. The remaining stamina gives you room to respond when an enemy turns or starts a second attack. See the [combat basics guide](/guides/combat-basics/) for a short practice routine.

<Callout type="tip" title="A useful habit">
End your first combo a hit early. A safe opening is more valuable than a small amount of extra damage.
</Callout>

## Pick a weapon you understand

The blade has a short recovery, the bow gives you distance, and the spear rewards careful positioning. The [weapon comparison](/equipment/weapon-tiers/) explains the tradeoffs in this imaginary balance model.

| Starting option | What it teaches | Main tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practice blade | Timing short openings | Limited reach |
| Oakbow | Spacing and patience | Vulnerable at close range |
| Traveler’s spear | Keeping an enemy at a distance | Longer recovery |

## Prepare at camp

Rest restores your supplies in this demo setting. Before leaving, check the following:

- Upgrade the weapon you actually plan to use.
- Put trail rations in a quick slot.
- Read the [Moss Guardian’s attack tells](/bosses/moss-guardian/).
- Open the [Vale map](/world/vale-map/) and locate the next campfire.

## Your next milestone

Follow the river north until you see roots crossing the path. The grove campfire is before the clearing, so you can practice the encounter without repeating the entire route.
