## The idea behind the build

Keep the equipment light, make one safe response at a time, and leave enough stamina to move. This loadout demonstrates a clear purpose without relying on a large collection of unexplained numerical bonuses.

## A simple skill sequence

1. Observe the enemy’s preparation.
2. Use Sidestep as the attack begins.
3. Answer with Patient Strike during recovery.
4. Return to neutral distance and let stamina recover.

## Where it works

The [Moss Guardian](/bosses/moss-guardian/) gives this sequence a readable test encounter. The build is less suited to prolonged ranged pressure, where the [Oakbow comparison](/equipment/weapon-tiers/) provides another option.
